Introduction

You Laugh You Lose is a challenge game: pick a funny YouTube video, keep a straight face, and see how long you can last. Your webcam watches for smiles and laughs using MediaPipe Face Landmarker blendshapes (mouthSmileLeft, mouthSmileRight, jawOpen).

All processing runs in your browser — no video is uploaded. A timeline is synced to the YouTube playback so you can see exactly when you broke.

How it works

  • MediaPipe Face Landmarker detects facial expressions from your webcam feed.
  • The YouTube IFrame API tracks video position and detects when the video ends.
  • MediaRecorder captures your face cam for replay on the results screen.
  • Laugh events are timestamped against the YouTube video's current time.
